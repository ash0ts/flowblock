# FlowBlock datasets and measured results

We acquired real product footage and ran the implemented SAM 2.1 Small worker on eight Objectron clips. The experiment produced inspectable measurements and exposed tracking failures that the current uncertainty rules missed. It does **not** establish mask accuracy, better shot planning in real rooms, or faster usable takes. The local results and a native Weave evaluation are verified. The separate Models publication failed.

**Dataset choice.** [Objectron](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Objectron) supplies mobile object videos, annotated 3D boxes and AR camera metadata under [C-UDA-1.0](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Objectron/blob/master/LICENSE). The [paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Ahmadyan_Objectron_A_Large_Scale_Dataset_of_Object-Centric_Videos_in_the_CVPR_2021_paper.html) describes manual box annotation. The sampled records do not identify individual annotators. A projected cuboid differs from the visible product silhouette, even with perfect segmentation; that distinction determines what we can measure.

Other reviewed sources remain useful for different questions:

**Acquisition and execution.** The fixed convenience sample selected the first two IDs from each Objectron test index for book, bottle, camera, cup and laptop. “Test” is the upstream filename: these are FlowBlock **development** clips, not fresh confirmation evidence. We retained all ten selected sequences and their acquisition failures.

- Downloaded **248,025,321 bytes**: ten MOVs, nine annotation protos, nine AR metadata files and five source indices. Exact URLs, hashes and errors are in the [acquisition readback](../../../artifacts/public-data/objectron/acquisition-readback.json).
- Excluded `bottle/batch-3/14` because its original duration is 3.965 seconds, below the worker's five-second minimum. Excluded `cup/batch-16/47` because its annotation could not be acquired. Neither was replaced or padded.
- Processed the first five seconds of each remaining video, preserving speed. All originals declare a -90° display rotation; reviewed annotation projections already use upright coordinates. Annotation timestamps are zero, so frame IDs map to original FFprobe presentation timestamps. Worker samples map to the nearest original frame within 0.018 seconds.
- Ran pinned SAM 2.1 Small locally on MPS with the existing fallback behavior. Only the first projected box was supplied as a prompt. Later reference labels were withheld from SAM; seed-frame measurements are excluded below. [Method and reproduction](../objectron.md), [executed runtime hashes](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/runtime/manifest.json), [cohort and exclusions](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/cohort.json).

Assistant inspection of midpoint/end overlays found apparent leakage onto a camera's neighboring price card, omission of a cup's visible white interior, and extension from a laptop toward nearby cables/table. [Frame/time/hash-linked notes](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/qualitative-inspection.json) preserve those observations. They are not independent ground-truth annotations and did not alter the scores. They demonstrate why 100% heuristic acceptance cannot be reported as 100% accurate tracking. The inspected footage and [contact sheet](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/tracking-contact-sheet.jpg) remain local.

**Planner data.** The separate [frozen planner protocol](../data/protocol.json) contains 64 training, 12 development and 30 confirmation scene groups, with six dependent movement tasks per group. The 1,032 teacher rows come from solver-checked witnesses and explicit unsupported requests; unresolved searches do not become infeasibility labels. These are new instances from shared synthetic topology templates, not unseen room families, real product identities or creator transfer. The v3 teacher-generation history remains frozen; the v4 execution checker removes an unstated 5% size-change requirement. All 468 positive compile targets passed an [independent v4 replay](independent-teacher-replay-v4.json). No confirmation results are claimed here.

**Forge status.** The [metadata-only Models package](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/publication/manifest.json) includes source hashes, attribution, product dimensions, membership and protocol. Its [results](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/publication/results.json) preserve all ten rows, including exclusions. A separate [Weave package](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/weave-publication/manifest.json) preserves the same outputs and explicitly identifies local MPS execution. Neither package contains raw footage, frames or pixel annotations.

Publication used the existing private `wandb/flowblock` project through `https://api.wandb.ai` after the Forge API route stalled. This imports the recorded local MPS results; no remote GPU execution is implied.

The separate Models publication **failed**. The [final readback](../../../artifacts/objectron-diagnostic-v1/models-final-shared-readback.json), completed after the last resume at 18:23:45 UTC, confirms that the [dataset producer](https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/wandb/flowblock/runs/dataset-6f88242a4b783333f96a92e4) failed with the expected manifest hash but zero logged artifacts; the benchmark consumer is absent. There is no Objectron Models results dashboard. All attempts and current states remain in the [publication status record](objectron-publication-status.json). No further service writes are being attempted in this pass.

**What remains unanswered.** Independent silhouette annotations are needed to evaluate tracking accuracy and calibrate review rules. The creator question requires a prospective, counterbalanced study of the existing workflow, a fixed recipe card and FlowBlock feedback, including an uncoached repeat to measure practice. Measure time from setup through the first usable take, including upload/annotation effort; use reviewers blind to the workflow and treat creators as the independent units. No reviewed public dataset contains that intervention and outcome, and this component diagnostic cannot substitute for it.
